# NX-021 — inverse consequence proposals
Use disjoint Forge validation panels from 11000–11319 and 12000–12319. On the common-fault stream compare veto only, inverse-map repair without confirmation, and repair confirmed by a second action outcome. Repeat with an action outcome sharing the original fault. Fit the consequence map from noisy cues and observations in a nominal calibration world. Report final accuracy across all addresses, accepted-write precision, coverage, probe use and paired seed differences. Primary criterion: improve final accuracy over veto by at least 10 percentage points and maintain 95% write precision on both independent-action panels. No criterion for the shared-channel negative control.
