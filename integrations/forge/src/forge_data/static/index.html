FROM SOURCE TO EXPERIMENT

A clearer path through your data.

  1. Bring a source. Upload chronological JSONL and describe its identity, version, provenance and use permissions. The constructed example requires no network or real-world records.
  2. Freeze the release. Forge validates the existing temporal schema, separates subjects into train/validation/TEST and pins exact file bytes. Every release gets its own identity.
  3. Explore what was available. Choose observation fields and a feature cutoff. Future labels remain hidden unless you explicitly choose a separate outcome cutoff. TEST rows are withheld in this GUI.
  4. Keep the evidence. Recheck integrity, compare release metadata, download the exact manifest and export selected train/validation examples as JSONL or spreadsheet-safe CSV.

Forge and TrainLab share hosting and interface conventions. When Forge is enabled in the TrainLab service, use the workbench switcher to move between datasets and experiments.

This is a shared operator workspace. Authentication, filesystem quotas, source permissions and deployment acceptance remain part of the production installation. Keep private sources within their authorized boundary.
