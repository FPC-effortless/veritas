Date: 1 November 2026. Scope: an incubated **local research operating path** around
TrainLab, not a qualified autonomous scientist or a new Veritas release.

## Observed execution

Three independent design-agent sessions supplied causal, robustness and efficiency
proposals. Their original JSON records are retained unchanged. Derived protocols
explicitly execute the exact-store engineering portions; proposed learned-model
comparisons remain unperformed.

The canonical campaign completed **24 tasks**:

- 18 primary executions: three protocols × three seeds × two declared arms;
- three separate-principal replication tasks containing 18 fresh executions;
- three reviews by a separate scientific-evidence agent/session.

All 36 canonical execution receipts succeeded. Every deterministic prediction file
was byte-identical on replay. Causal and robustness scores matched exactly;
efficiency's largest numeric score difference was 0.006990739, within its frozen
absolute tolerance of 0.20. Timing is not claimed to be deterministic.

Efficiency's external control score is a bookkeeping zero. The meaningful timing
comparison is **inside each invocation**, between retained execution and repeated
prefix replay with correctness checked independently. This is not an optimized
cache, neural-model, equal-memory, energy or dollar-saving comparison.

The controller was dropped and reopened after the first primary job. The remaining
queue completed from durable state without manually transferring the proposals'
scientific context between workers. All scientific qualification fields remain

its explicitly constructed engineering scope. **Model fits: zero. Paid compute:
none. External scientific replication: not performed.**

## Independent audits

The replication operator did not design or implement the fixtures. It executed all
three replay tasks through the controller, checked all seed/arm cells, confirmed
disjoint original/replay run IDs and checked clean-directory snapshot inventories.

The evidence reviewer did not design, implement or execute the primary/replay
work. It independently reconstructed targets, category/cell summaries, paired
effects, bootstrap arithmetic, operation counts and timing summaries from all 36
saved receipts/results, then submitted three exact-evidence reviews through the
controller. It performed no new optimizer fits or measured benchmark executions.

The implementation reviewer initially found six blockers: post-success indexing,
unchecked snapshot changes, omitted failed attempts, author-alias self-review,
whole-tree artifact copying and invalid review admission. Those defects were fixed
and regressions added. The reviewer subsequently reproduced rejection of a changed
snapshot after a completed review and confirmed no partial packet was published.
Exact-head GitHub review remains visible on the PR and becomes stale on any new
head, independently of these historical observations.

Agent-session independence is an operational assertion through one account, not a
claim of distinct cryptographic identities or an external laboratory.

- Ruff, formatting and Mypy checks passed;

Forge quickstart launched a subprocess from another working directory. Absolute
workspace paths repair that configuration. Python 3.13 also surfaced unclosed
SQLite context warnings; connection contexts now close explicitly, with a
regression. Neither repair changes the frozen diagnostic fixtures.

This record does not assert that all repository, release, device, commercial or
external-runtime qualification gates have passed.
