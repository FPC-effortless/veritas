## Boundaries shared across 082–086

World processing uses local Veritas LifeWorld, per-role port, immutable visible observations, and inert authorized probe receipts. Host generator seed, hidden nuisance, legal/adjudication truth, inaccessible state, and held answers are outside the fit or predictor workspace. Distinct roles: acquisition-only training material; query-only prediction material; evaluator-only reference and outputs. Training outcomes are only consequences allowed in the simulated agent's observation stream, not direct target assignments from an evaluator. Correlated sample counts do not increase independent seed count. Scope excludes real people, legal advice/decisions, money, deployed actions, protected TEST, and claims about human-like intelligence.

### 084: local randomized interventions on safe paired synthetic branches

160 paired opportunities before a mechanism change and 160 after, each seed. Latent nuisance is balanced and independently shuffled within each phase, and randomized setting is balanced across the hidden strata. Stratum is not supplied as a learner field or encoded by sequence parity. Pair identities are opaque; latent correlates can still exist in the observed outcomes by design. Active branch obtains one native local inert probe at each opportunity; passive branch observes a nuisance-correlated setting; frozen uses the same early observational estimates without post-change learning. Both active and passive have the same number of opportunities and updates, but active pays one checked probe per opportunity. Count exposures and probes, actual process wall separately, no hidden cost comparison claim. Model is a smoothed binary difference in conditional frequencies; no fitted observation classifier or action oracle. Early and late adaptive arms summarize the current phase only. Frozen first-phase control estimates old environment.

Gold is the finite population contrast in 160 independent modeled nuisance worlds per phase, generated from separately namespaced draws under the same mechanism. A/B learning sees only one realized branch and no held-world outcomes. Compare absolute error and sign after shift. Require >=20 observations per setting per arm, post-shift probe error at least .10 smaller than the better passive/frozen control and correct sign in each seed before calling a directional pilot benefit. The simulation intentionally supplies known benefits of randomization when a confounder exists. A positive pilot does not establish autonomous causal discovery, general SCM inference, or cheaper full operation.

### 086: replay versus current-only and uniform history

256 observed practices per seed, early and interfering late (128 each), two distinct observable cue tokens, same current exposure order for all three arms. Every arm takes exactly two updates per example, 512 updates and 1,536 feature touches. Nonreplay spends second update on current observation; uniform chooses an earlier capacity-32 memory entry with fixed seed; selective picks highest present loss on past entries only. First item repeated in all arms. Acquisition exposure and initial model fixed. Distinct evaluation world identifiers, 16 query entries per phase per seed with eight recurring cue motifs repeated twice; do not treat those entries as independent or call novel language transfer. Compare delayed accuracy and Brier after interference, old-vs-early change, late retention, uniform comparison, retrieval work, and process/replay costs. Primary directional gate: selective improves old Brier at least .02 against current in every seed; late Brier must not be more than .05 worse in any seed. Uniform descriptive comparison is not evidence that selection is better than uniform absent a separate planned criterion. Selection overhead is real, not matched merely by training update count. No cost superiority or promotion from this pilot.
