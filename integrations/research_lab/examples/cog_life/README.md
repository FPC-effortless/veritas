# COG-LIFE × COG-ATTN 081–090 campaign

A CPU-only staged Veritas research campaign on constructed developmental worlds. All
legal rules, persons, institutions and actions are fictional. No real-party data,
live legal advice, external action, paid resources, or protected TEST access.
A completed implementation or successful native receipt is not scientific qualification.

The life engine lives under `src/investigation_world/life`. The actor only sees its
scoped observations and receipts; source events and adjudication are separate.
World history, simulator random state, learner state, and policy permissions are
separate and require their own reproducibility checks.

Before any fit, freeze world IDs plus transitive lineage, layouts, regime, seed
allocation, comparators, absolute support floors per important group, run limits,
payload schema, token/experience and update accounting, and score rules. TRAIN,
CAL and held DEV world families are disjoint; challenge siblings remain paired
within their fold. Realized support must be checked without predictions. Control
interfaces not compatible with the pinned historical checkpoint are not papered
over using a substitute with the same name. A historical diagnostic is separate
from a fair matched comparison.

The conductor is not an admission decision. It checks exact independently
reviewed byte manifests and native criteria and retains stage failures. Bundle
production and held scoring are separately reviewed. The scientific evaluator,
not the policy, owns hidden DEV truth and verifies results and authorized effects
from the retained records. Record scientific status separately for each of the
ten studies, including deferred and blocked endpoints.
