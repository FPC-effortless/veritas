# COG-LIFE × COG-ATTN 081–090: first Veritas campaign

**No integrated developmental-intelligence qualification.** Native replayable worlds were built and bounded components and curriculum comparisons ran. This did not test a single continuously learning agent from childhood to professional life. All cases and actions were fictional. Independent reviews reconciled retained outputs and native receipts, not external replication.

| Study | Audited outcome | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 081 Persistent world | 8 native lives, 280 events, 8 matching same-process simulator save/load replays, 16/16 boundary denials. | Engineering result, not learner recovery. |
| 082 Grounding | 288/288 supported phrases; 96/96 unsupported abstentions; 192/192 goals with the learned effect catalog vs 0/192 under each limited-effect control. | Held wording reused trained cues; effects came from a host-provided synthetic transition oracle. Not open language. |
