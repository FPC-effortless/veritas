# Capability Development Pricing and Positioning

This document defines positioning discipline for early Veritas commercial engagements. It is not a price guarantee, invoice, or legal offer.

## Sell the decision, not the architecture

Price against the scope and value of the operational decision being resolved, not the number of Veritas tasks, schemas, world objects, or internal components used.

The core offer is:

> Independent qualification of one consequential AI capability against executable operational state, followed by evidence-backed failure analysis and a clear deployment or improvement decision.

## Initial offer structure

### Qualification Pilot

Use a fixed-scope pilot when the integration and workflow boundary are known.

The current Voice Operations program may use the existing **$5,000 fixed-scope target** defined by its product issue as an initial market test. That amount is a positioning hypothesis, not proof of market-clearing price.

A fixed-scope quote should state:

- one target workflow/capability;
- agreed number/version of private scenarios or one frozen panel identity;
- number of evaluated model/agent configurations;
- repeated-run policy;
- integration assumptions;
- buyer-safe report and readout;
- whether a rerunnable regression suite is included;
- inference/third-party compute treatment;
- exclusions and change-control triggers.

Do not discount by adding unbounded architecture or custom-world work into a fixed pilot.

### Capability Improvement Cycle

Quote separately when the buyer wants a second intervention condition and held-out reevaluation. Price should reflect additional environment/panel work, repeated evaluation, failure analysis, and regression evidence rather than presenting it as a free rerun.

### Verified Training-Value Program

Do not sell a guaranteed training improvement. Scope and price the controlled experiment: baseline, training bundle construction where authorized, external training integration, held-out reevaluation, multiple seeds where required, exploit monitoring, OOD/structural transfer, and regression evidence.

The commercial promise is a rigorous answer to whether the intervention worked under the declared protocol.

### Learning-Efficiency Program

Do not sell a guaranteed cost saving before matched-budget evidence exists. Scope resource accounting, control/treatment construction, external training, replicated evaluation, and held-out capability analysis.

A positive result may support a later stronger value-based price. A negative result remains valid experiment output.

## Variables that justify a higher quote

- private/custom world construction rather than reuse of a qualified pack;
- live/native system integration complexity;
- many model/harness comparison conditions;
- repeated stochastic runs;
- stringent privacy/confidentiality controls;
- sealed or fresh held-out panel generation;
- adversarial/red-team depth;
- long-horizon or expensive runtime episodes;
- specialized expert review;
- customer-specific reporting/procurement requirements;
- training experiment execution and resource accounting;
- ongoing regression monitoring rather than a one-time decision.

## Variables that should not be used as value theater

Do not justify price primarily with:

- raw episode count;
- number of internal Veritas modules;
- number of supported adapters;
- synthetic token/task volume;
- claims of proprietary intelligence unsupported by buyer evidence.

## Claim boundaries in sales material

Permitted when supported by the exact engagement evidence:

- the evaluated system met or failed a declared threshold;
- a configuration outperformed another on the frozen comparison;
- a particular failure mechanism was observed;
- an intervention improved held-out capability under the declared comparison;
- a defined authority envelope is or is not supported by the evidence.

Require dedicated qualification before claiming:

- scientific validity beyond the applicable qualified environment;
- frontier-model utility;
- training validation;
- broad structural/OOD transfer;
- Veritas-directed learning efficiency superiority;
- production suitability outside the evaluated operational envelope.

Never claim from estimated or absent evidence:

- actual customer cost savings;
- observed GPU or human-effort savings;
- universal model rankings;
- guaranteed training gain;
- guaranteed deployment safety.

## Early commercial learning targets

For the first design partners, collect evidence about:

- which capability framing produces a concrete buyer response;
- what buyers consider a material deployment threshold;
- which deliverable drives the purchase decision;
- actual integration effort;
- willingness to pay for one-time qualification versus ongoing regression monitoring;
- whether failure diagnosis leads to a customer intervention;
- whether the intervention outcome creates expansion demand.

Do not over-optimize pricing before this evidence exists. The first objective is to establish repeatable willingness to pay for a concrete Veritas decision product.
