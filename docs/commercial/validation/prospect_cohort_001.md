## Priority interpretation
The first eight are the highest-priority research targets because they are closest to the agent-evaluation/reliability problem. They are also the hardest differentiation tests: Braintrust and LangSmith already offer continuous agent evaluation/observability, while Decagon and Sierra operate agent systems with substantial testing/QA capabilities. This is intentional. If Veritas cannot produce incremental value for sophisticated evaluation teams, that is important negative evidence.

Current public product materials support this fit assessment: Braintrust emphasizes trace-level scoring, evals, observability and release gates; LangSmith emphasizes offline/online evaluation and continuous agent development; Decagon describes simulations, checkpoints and continuous testing; Rasa emphasizes reliable enterprise agents and control; Sierra publishes research on real-world agent evaluation. These sources establish category relevance, not customer intent or willingness to buy.
