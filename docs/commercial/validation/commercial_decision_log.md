# Commercial Decision Log

## Pre-registered hypothesis
An AI-agent developer/operator will provide a real operational workflow for an independent Veritas capability audit and will seek repeat evaluation or pay for continued qualification when actionable failures affect a deployment or operational decision.

## Cohort
Approximately 20 highly qualified prospects.

## GREEN
- >=8 provide a concrete workflow.
- >=5 permit evaluation.
- >=3 experience a materially useful failure finding.
- >=2 request repeat evaluation.
- >=1 pays for a qualification pilot.

## YELLOW
Real workflow/evaluation use and useful failures are demonstrated, but payment or repeat behavior is insufficient.

Response: test buyer, scope, pricing, procurement path and urgency before expanding engineering.

## RED
Qualified prospects understand the proposition but consistently refuse workflow/agent access; evaluations fail to differentiate; findings do not affect decisions; or repeat evaluation has no pull.

Response: revisit the capability wedge and/or product proposition rather than adding environments/features by default.
