# Veritas Design-Partner Contracting Checklist

Status: checklist for owner/counsel review. It is not a substitute for legal advice and does not itself create contractual terms.

## NDA

Confirm:
- mutual or one-way confidentiality scope;
- private benchmark worlds, seeds, oracles and exploit suites are expressly confidential;
- customer model/harness details and evaluation results are confidential;
- permitted disclosure to employees/contractors on a need-to-know basis;
- residuals/no-reverse-engineering treatment appropriate to private benchmark material;
- standard exclusions and compelled-disclosure process.

## MSA

Confirm:
- contracting legal entity and addresses;
- services description and SOW precedence;
- fees, taxes, invoicing and payment terms;
- IP ownership of pre-existing Veritas technology;
- rights in customer materials and customer-specific adapters;
- rights in evaluation reports and aggregate/anonymized metrics;
- confidentiality;
- security obligations and incident notification;
- warranties/disclaimers appropriate for a synthetic evaluation service;
- limitation of liability and excluded damages;
- indemnities, if any;
- term/termination and effect of termination;
- governing law/dispute mechanism;
- assignment, publicity, subcontracting and notices.

## SOW / pilot order form

Freeze:
- evaluated model/agent/harness version;
- endpoint/container/CLI integration method;
- benchmark version and private run identifier;
- permitted tools and credentials;
- token/tool/time/retry budgets;
- number of configurations and attempts;
- evaluation levels and task-family sample;
- inference/third-party cost treatment;
- delivery dates and report format;
- one included controlled re-test, if offered;
- acceptance criterion tied to a customer decision rather than a guaranteed score;
- explicit statement that synthetic benchmark performance is not a warranty of production behavior.

## DPA

Use only when Veritas processes personal data on the customer's behalf. Confirm:
- controller/processor roles;
- subject matter, duration, purpose and categories of data/data subjects;
- documented instructions;
- confidentiality obligations;
- security measures;
- subprocessors and change mechanism;
- assistance with rights requests and breach obligations;
- deletion/return at termination;
- audit/information rights;
- international transfer mechanism if needed.

Prefer pilots that use synthetic data and customer-controlled inference so a DPA may be unnecessary or minimal.

## Security attachment

Attach or reference:
- evaluation architecture/data-flow diagram;
- retention policy;
- incident-response policy;
- subprocessor register for the actual deployment;
- dependency/security scan evidence;
- credential-handling practices;
- isolation/network policy;
- current limitations: no SOC 2 or penetration-test claim unless actually completed.

## Publicity / case study

For discounted design partners, negotiate separately whether Veritas may publish:
- customer name/logo;
- anonymized score deltas;
- methodology description;
- named case study;
- quotes.

Default should remain confidential unless affirmative written permission is granted.
