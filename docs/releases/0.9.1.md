# Veritas 0.9.1 — Experimental Integrity

Veritas 0.9.1 hardens the measurement contracts underneath CompanyWorld, ProjectWorld, and the Continuous Agent Capability Observatory before additional environment realism is added.

## Integrity guarantees

- CompanyWorld reward is grounded in admissible evidence. Oracle-correct but unsupported claims remain visible diagnostically but do not earn task reward.
- Adversarial coverage includes unsupported oracle-correct answers, misleading system projections, non-entailing related evidence, and poisoned evidence candidates.
- Longitudinal aggregate drift is computed on matched scenario/task/scenario-seed panels and reports panel coverage/dropout.
- Model × intervention effects require identical paired scenario panels and use paired difference-in-differences.
- Small-sample confidence intervals use Student-t critical values.
- ProjectWorld exposes an identity-bound model-facing session so an action cannot self-assign a different role.
- Intervention semantics distinguish `semantic_truth_preserved` from `evidence_solvability_preserved`.
- Tool-failure scope distinguishes system, aggregator, and global outages.
- Recovery matching is resource/system specific rather than method-name only.
- Cadence scheduling implements catch-up semantics and an exclusive expiring shared-filesystem lease.
- Model-generation RNG replicates are available through `observatory.generation_replicates` and require identical scenario panels. Providers without an explicit seed contract are rejected rather than silently treated as seeded.

## Training Value v3 evidence

The completed replicated Training Value v3 matrix ran on the hardened verifier with the same frozen 24-example training panel and 100-episode held-out panel across independent training seeds 7, 17, and 29.

### Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct

- mean held-out paired improvement: **+0.5628**;
- seed-level Student-t 95% interval: **[0.4220, 0.7036]**;
- 87% of held-out episodes improved in each replicate;
- all aggregate panel-identity checks passed.

### Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct

- mean held-out paired improvement: **+0.7562**;
- seed-level Student-t 95% interval: **[0.7182, 0.7941]**;
- 100% of held-out episodes improved across all three replicates;
- all aggregate panel-identity checks passed.

These results establish reproducible within-family transfer on generated CompanyWorld diagnostic tasks. They do **not** by themselves establish broad cross-domain or structurally out-of-distribution agent-capability transfer; stronger transfer claims require source/grammar-disjoint training and evaluation families.

The earlier hardened 12-example smoke result (+0.0875 on 12 held-out episodes, concentrated in 2/12 cases with a confidence interval including zero) is retained only as historical continuity with the pre-replication experiment.

The expensive six-run replicated matrix is manual-only after this release. Ordinary pull requests run the hardened smoke when Training Value/verifier paths change.
