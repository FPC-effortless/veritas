# grill-me
Research searchable facts yourself. For each high-leverage decision give the recommendation, strongest countercase, consequences, and what evidence would change the recommendation. For benchmark/RL ideas test verifier loopholes, leakage, contamination, saturation, weak discrimination, and unfalsifiable claims. Novelty is not evidence.
