# triage
Determine category, reproducibility, duplicates, blockers, security/privacy/scientific impact, required evidence, and authority. Distinguish `needs-info`, `ready-for-agent`, `ready-for-human`, and `wontfix` as appropriate. Do not silently expand an approved release/task contract.
