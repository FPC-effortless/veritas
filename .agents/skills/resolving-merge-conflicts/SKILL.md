# resolving-merge-conflicts
Inspect base and both sides. Identify governing issue/spec and active file ownership. Preserve compatible behavior from both sides; never choose by recency alone. Do not silently drop tests, scientific/privacy gates, or evidence. Re-run targeted and broader checks after resolution. Escalate genuinely incompatible policy decisions.
