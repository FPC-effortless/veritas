# wayfinder
Do not implement. Map decisions, assumptions, evidence needed, alternatives, reversibility, dependencies, and authority. For ML/RL/benchmark work include capability/hypothesis, data/ground-truth constraints, verifier, compute feasibility, baselines, leakage/privacy risks, and qualification path. Collapse the map until a coherent route is ready for `to-spec`.
